# FAQ

**Why is the name weighted highest?**
Name is the strongest text-match field in GitHub search, so it carries the
largest share of the score (25%). Keep the exact phrase people search for in
the repository name.

**My text score is perfect - why isn't the grade an A?**
Stars (20%) and activity (10%) are the remaining 30% of the total. With a
perfect text score and no stars, the best possible grade is a B.

**How often should I re-run the audit?**
After every change to the name, description, topics, or README. Otherwise
once a month is enough - activity decays slowly on the 0-7 / 8-30 day bands.
